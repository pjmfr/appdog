Meet and Handoff Sheet

For the person giving the dog away. Run through it with the adopter, in order. Tap an item when it is done.

1. Before you meet

2. Ask them

Tap only when you got a clear, believable answer.

3. Stop if you see this

Tap anything you notice. One tap puts the sheet on STOP.

4. The handoff

By typing both names, the adopter agrees to:

  1. Never breed, fight or resell the dog.
  2. Return the dog to the owner or a rescue if they cannot keep it.
  3. Keep the dog vaccinated and see a vet when needed.
  4. Answer a few check-in messages from the owner.

Keep a record
